## June 26–28, 2026 | Twin Cities, Minnesota

Welcome to the 2026 Deaf Midwest Pickleball Tournament!

Join Deaf and Hard of Hearing pickleball players from across the Midwest for a weekend of competition, community, and fun in the Twin Cities.

This tournament celebrates athletic skill, sportsmanship, and Deaf culture. Players of all skill levels are welcome. Whether you’re competing, supporting, or volunteering, we’re excited to have you with us!

![Sculpture of a spoon with cherry on it on a small pond. City of Minneapolis in the background. Text: "Minneapolis: City of Lakes"](images/minneapolis-1024x683.png)

## Tournament Location

The event will be held at [Chip's Pickleball Club](https://www.chipspickleball.com/).

980 Discovery Road, Eagan, MN 55121.

[![](images/o-300x225.jpg)](https://www.chipspickleball.com/)

Established in 2023, Chip’s Pickleball Club is an indoor facility featuring 12 tournament-sized courts, a sauna, and a cold plunge for recovery purposes. It also features a cafe and bar where you can gather and spend time together.

## Date

The tournament will be held during the weekend of June 26 - 28, 2026.
