**What is the Deaf Midwest Pickleball Tournament?**The Deaf Midwest Pickleball Tournament is a regional pickleball competition for Deaf and Hard of Hearing players. It focuses on accessibility, sportsmanship, and community connection.

* * *

**When is the tournament?**The tournament will take place Friday through Sunday, June 26–28, 2026.

* * *

**Where is the tournament located?**The tournament will be held in the Twin Cities, Minnesota. Our venue will be Chip’s Pickleball Club, located at 980 Discovery Road, Eagan, MN 55121.

* * *

**Who can participate?**Deaf and Hard of Hearing pickleball players are welcome. All skill levels are encouraged to participate.

* * *

**Do I need to know ASL to participate?**ASL is the primary language used during the tournament. However, we welcome all Deaf and Hard of Hearing participants and will prioritize clear visual communication.

* * *

**What accommodations are nearby?**Eagan’s hotel choices sit along major roads and near Minneapolis-Saint Paul International Airport (MSP), about a 10–20 minute drive from Chip’s Pickleball Club. Booking early is recommended, especially around tournament weekends or events in June.

* * *

**Will there be spectators?**Yes! Spectators, family members, and friends are welcome to attend and support the players.

* * *

**What accessibility features will be provided?**The tournament will include ASL-friendly communication, visual announcements, clear signage, and staff/volunteers familiar with Deaf culture.

* * *

**Can I volunteer?**Yes! Volunteers are welcome and appreciated. Volunteer sign-up information will be posted soon. No pickleball experience is required.

* * *

**How can I stay updated?**Follow us on social media and check this website for announcements, registration updates, and schedule information.
