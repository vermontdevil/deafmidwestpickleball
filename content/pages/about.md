The Deaf Midwest Pickleball Tournament is an annual event bringing together Deaf and Hard of Hearing athletes from across the region. Established in 2021, the inaugural tournament took place in Chicago, Illinois. Since then, it has been to Indianapolis, St. Louis, and more. The 2026 tournament will be hosted in the Twin Cities during the last weekend of June.

Our mission is to:

- Promote pickleball within the Deaf community

- Provide an accessible and inclusive competitive environment

- Build connections and friendships through sport
