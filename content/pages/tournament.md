## Schedule - 3 action-packed days!

**Friday, June 26**

- 6:00 pm – 10:00 pm: Friday Night Cash Clash – A fun, competitive cash event with fixed partners.

**Saturday, June 27**

- 8:00 am – 11:30 am: Women’s Doubles

- 12:30 pm – 4:00 pm: Men’s Doubles

**Sunday, June 28**

- 8:00 am – 4:00 pm: Mixed Doubles – Teams of one man and one woman

## Format

- Skill Levels: We have groups for 3.0, 3.5, and 4.0 players.

- The Games: Everyone plays "Round Robin" style (you play against many teams). Games go up to 11 points.

- Playoffs: The top 3 teams in each group move on to the finals!

\*Only Saturday/Sunday doubles events will be reported to DUPR. Friday Night Cash Clash will not count towards your DUPR score.

## Cost

The more events you play, the more you save:

- 1st Event: $75

- 2nd Event: Additional $10

- Friday Cash Clash is $35 (Must also be registered for either Saturday and/or Sunday event)

- No refunds after May 31, 2026.

## 2023 Tournament Results

We have completed our second Deaf Midwest Pickleball Tournament on August 12, 2023 in Indianapolis, Indiana. 107 players signed up to play at West Indy Racquet Club. We played Men doubles, Women doubles, and Mixed doubles. It was a wonderful experience despite the heat, rain delays, and a false fire alarm!

You can see all of our [medalists and participants on Pickleball Brackets](https://pickleballbrackets.com/ptplg.aspx?eid=1f9a5638-4944-4927-b3f4-cd6c048ec73d). A big _hands waving_ to everyone who came to play and had a blast!

You can see the recording of the [livestream video of the tournament via Streamyard](https://www.facebook.com/793958674/videos/195671503496650/) thanks to [Indy Deaf Pickleball (Justin Perez, Sebastien Ianno, and Jake Schwall)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1802484819925445/?__cft__[0]=AZUsqCtsZ8BaD3xxg7gtKqGU3B3pHT2ZsT4uUx6tvtFy_57g3SjjvjuhmI8Gv-IoWxPX5hrG6xT9SZkWaz_4QGQtjqFKYpEKcn34xHlSiGtbNIhLpE4Bfq3UAqRqhTRV4mvmzWejL0stuu4WoxexzPTqIQtcxeqVAmeKSgnqnVmleWeq_iVVsWwiQ_FKoY-GpblpMV3pVKpG12xarRTF-IQ2&__tn__=-UK-y-R).

Lastly, without our wonderful sponsors: Heritage Interpreting, Indiana Relay, Selkirk, Gearbox, and Franklin, our tournament wouldn't have been done! Thank you to the sponsors!
